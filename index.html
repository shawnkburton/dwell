Where the details come from

This is a local, responsive HTML prototype with 10 selected homes around the Gonzales / Fairfield area near 1920 Crescent Road, plus the adjoining Rockland area. It is not a live listing feed or a native mobile app.

Prices, bedrooms, bathrooms, listed floor areas, lot sizes, construction years, annual tax figures and older sales come from the linked REALTOR.ca listings. Descriptions are original summaries. Three actual listing photos are linked per home; an internet connection is needed to load them. Photos may include staging or digital alteration by the listing provider.

2026 assessed values are from BC Assessment, with a valuation date of July 1, 2025. Assessment is not an asking price, sale price or tax bill. Annual property taxes shown here are listing-reported figures; their tax years were not specified in the accessed listing fields and have not been verified against municipal tax notices.

BC Assessment’s recent-sale section covers its stated “last 3 full calendar years.” An empty recent-sale record does not mean the home has never sold. Older REALTOR.ca sale records are displayed separately, with any source conflicts noted.

Source checks: September 25, 2026 (Victoria time). Search-index copies can lag live pages. Reconfirm price, availability, measurements and permitted suites before relying on them.

BC Assessment states that commercial reuse requires prior written authorization. This review prototype is not a licensed commercial listing service. Production deployment needs authorized listing/photo feeds and appropriate assessment-data permissions.
